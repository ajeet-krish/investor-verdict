# 2 Minute Demo Script: investor-verdict

Total: 2 min. Roles: 1 presenter plus live app. Panel: Market Analyst, Product Manager, Shark, Mentor. All scores start at 50.

## 0:00 to 0:20 Intro (say verbatim)

Ever dreamed of being a Series A founder raising $5 million? Now you can larp as a founder and try to raise funding for your startup idea. Face a panel of investors in a Teams style call, where you pitch to 4 live, conversational agents. Every clear answer moves you closer to an offer, while every vague answer may cause an investor to back up entirely.

[CUE: share screen on call grid with 4 investor tiles plus founder tile, point to score bars at 50]

## 0:20 to 0:45 Bad pitch: vague hype loses

[CUE: speak into mic, then pause 1.5s for replies, point to bars falling live]

Founder says: My startup is amazing, everyone needs this, huge market, trust me, we will grow fast and crush it.

App responds with max 2 replies per turn, sequential single voice:

1. Shark, hardest yes: What is your price and margin today?
2. Market Analyst, hardest on vague numbers: Who pays what price each month?

[CUE: point to bars dropping from 50 toward 0, call out Shark and Market Analyst falling fastest]
Result: vague with no numbers, scores fall, at 0 the investor leaves the call.

## 0:45 to 1:15 Good pitch: specific numbers win

[CUE: stay on call grid, speak clearly, pause 1.5s, point to bars rising live]

Founder says: PantryPal for repair shops. 12 repair shops paid $5 last month. 10 weekly active. $2k MRR. CAC $40 LTV $600. Node backend 200ms p95. Asking $5 million Series A to add 50 shops in 6 months.

App responds with max 2 replies per turn, sequential single voice:

1. Mentor, easiest yes: Love the shop story, who loves you most?
2. Shark respects profit: $600 LTV on $40 CAC, what is payback?

[CUE: point to all bars rising toward 100, note Mentor leads, Shark nods on profit]
Result: specific with proof, scores climb, at 100 the investor makes a fictional offer.

Rule recap: at most 2 replies per turn, one voice at a time, scores move live, 0 leaves, 100 offers.

## 1:15 to 1:50 Afterthoughts and close

We want to expand to more diverse agent personalities or industry experts such as science or law.
We also want to expand into job interview prep tool.

[CUE: stop share, face camera]
Judges, come larp as a founder and try the live pitch now.
