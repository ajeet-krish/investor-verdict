# Verdict: two-minute founder roleplay demo

Goal: show a complete founder exchange and explain all six rubric categories. One presenter operates the live website. Questions and score changes are dynamic; do not promise an exact reply, fixed response time, or an offer within two minutes.

| Time | Say and do | Rubric evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | “You are a founder entering an investor meeting. Verdict lets you rehearse that pressure with four fictional AI investors.” Open the live site and Join. | Theme, ease of use |
| 0:15–0:35 | Identify The CTO, The VP, The Interrogator, and The Sympathizer. Select PantryPal and show that the founder can edit the pitch. Turn on the mic if venue audio permits. | Creativity, ease of use |
| 0:35–1:10 | Deliver the pitch below. Let the selected investors finish. Point to the speaking icon and confidence changes. | Working product, creativity |
| 1:10–1:35 | Answer the actual question using the fixed case facts. If asked about one-month retention, acknowledge that it is unknown after a two-week pilot. Show the suggested replies as optional help. | Usefulness, working product |
| 1:35–1:50 | Toggle Transcript: the history hides, but the message box stays. Explain that 0 triggers a farewell and departure; 100 triggers a simulated offer with Accept/Decline. | Ease of use, theme |
| 1:50–2:00 | “The goal is to practise clear answers and find missing evidence before a real meeting. Try being the founder yourself.” | Usefulness, demo |

## Opening pitch

“In this fictional practice case, I'm building PantryPal to help working parents waste less food with weekly meal plans. We interviewed 12 parents, and eight said they would pay $5 a month. In our two-week pilot with 20 households, five actually paid. We have early demand evidence, but we haven't proved long-term retention.”

## Example follow-up, only if asked about retention

“We don't know one-month retention yet because the pilot lasted two weeks. Our next step is a four-week trial to measure weekly use and paid renewal.”

Use the [fixed case facts](../agents/server/practice-cases.mjs) for other answers. Do not invent revenue, customer-acquisition costs, technical performance, or investment terms. The practice scenario describes families and meal planning, not repair shops.

## Rehearsal checks

- Test one complete live investor round, including audible playback and microphone transcription, before presenting.
- Confirm provider credentials, agent access, credits, and capacity for four persistent conversations.
- Prepare text input for a noisy venue. If audio fails, explain the failure honestly and show the actual text responses.
- Keep any prepared outcome recording clearly labelled as a recording. Do not claim a 0/100 ending happened live if it did not.
- Do not spend the demo waiting for a specific investor or trying to force an offer. A complete pitch and follow-up is the primary demonstration.
